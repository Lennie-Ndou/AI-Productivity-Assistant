# AI Workplace Productivity Assistant

Automate your daily work with structured AI assistance.

Generate professional emails, summarize meetings, plan tasks, conduct research, and manage your workflow from a single AI-powered workspace.

## Overview

AI Workplace Productivity Assistant is a web-based productivity platform designed to help professionals streamline their daily work using artificial intelligence.

The platform combines essential workplace tools into one centralized workspace, reducing time spent on repetitive tasks and helping users focus on high-value work.

## Features

### Smart Email Generator

Draft professional and polished emails tailored to your audience and communication style.

**Capabilities:**

- Professional email creation
- Tone customization
- Audience-specific messaging
- Business communication assistance
- Improved clarity and structure

### Meeting Notes Summarizer

Transform meeting notes into concise and actionable summaries.

**Capabilities:**

- Key point extraction
- Decision tracking

- Meeting recap generation
- Organized summary output

### AI Task Planner

Plan and prioritize your day using productivity best practices.

**Capabilities:**

### AI Research Assistant

### AI Chatbot

A conversational AI assistant designed to support workplace productivity.

**Capabilities:**
